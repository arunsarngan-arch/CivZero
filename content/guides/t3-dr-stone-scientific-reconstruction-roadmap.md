Millennia later, high school prodigy Senku Ishigami awakens into a pristine Stone World where every trace of human technology has crumbled to dust. ### Section 1: The First Step — Nitric Acid & The Revival Fluid

In the narrative, depetrification requires a powerful chemical agent capable of etching away the mineralized nitric-silicate crust that binds human cellular tissue. Senku identifies the solution: **Nital**, an industrial etching mixture of concentrated nitric acid and alcohol.

```
               THE NITAL SYNTHESIS PATHWAY
               
    Bat Guano in Limestone Cave         Wild Grapes / Sugar Cane
                 │                                  │
                 ▼                                  ▼
      Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)            Yeast Wine Fermentation
                 │                                  │
                 ▼ (+ H2SO4 Distillation)           ▼ (Pot Distillation)
        Nitric Acid (HNO3)                 Pure Ethanol (C2H5OH, 95%)
                 │                                  │
                 └───────────────┬──────────────────┘
                                 ▼
                      NITAL ETCHING SOLUTION
                 (Rapid Silicate Depetrification)
```

#### 1. Harvesting Natural Nitrates (Cave Guano)
- **The Biological Precursor**: Deep in limestone caves sheltered from rain, centuries of bat guano undergo bacterial nitration. Microbes oxidize organic urea and ammonia into potassium and calcium nitrates ($KNO_3$ and $Ca(NO_3)_2$).
- **Extraction Protocol**: Leach the dry, powdery guano through boiling water and wood-ash (potassium carbonate, $K_2CO_3$). Filter out organic insolubles. When the filtrate cools, crystalline **saltpeter (potassium nitrate, $KNO_3$)** precipitates out of solution.

#### 2. Distilling Nitric Acid ($HNO_3$)
- When potassium nitrate crystals are mixed with concentrated sulfuric acid ($H_2SO_4$) in a heat-resistant glass retort and heated:
  $$KNO_3 + H_2SO_4 \xrightarrow{\Delta} KHSO_4 + HNO_3 \uparrow$$
- Gaseous nitric acid boils off at $83^\circ\text{C}$, travels down a water-cooled condensing neck, and collects as fuming, pale yellow nitric acid.

#### 3. Fractional Distillation of Ethanol ($C_2H_5OH$)
- Wild grapes or starchy roots are fermented with wild yeast (*Saccharomyces cerevisiae*) into crude alcohol ($8\text{--}12\%$ ethanol).
- Boiling the fermented mash in a closed pot with a bamboo or copper condensing tube exploits the difference in boiling points: ethanol boils at **$78.37^\circ\text{C}$**, while water boils at **$100^\circ\text{C}$**.
- Repeated multi-stage fractional redistillation yields pure **95.6% azeotropic ethanol**. Blending 1 part concentrated nitric acid with 20 parts 95% ethanol yields **Nital**, the corrosive solvent used in metallurgy to etch metal crystalline grains.

### Section 2: Primitive Glassmaking & The Laboratory Foundation

In *Dr. STONE*, Senku emphasizes an unyielding reality: **chemistry cannot advance without transparent, chemically inert, heat-resistant glassware**. Earthenware pottery dissolves under concentrated sulfuric and nitric acid; only silica glass allows observation of exothermic reactions and acid distillation.

```
                  PRIMITIVE GLASSMAKING FLUX FORMULA
                  
   SILICA SAND (SiO2)       CALCIUM CARBONATE (CaCO3)    SODA ASH (Na2CO3)
   Quartz River Sand        Crushed Sea Shells           Burned Seaweed / Ash
        [ 70% ]                    [ 10% ]                      [ 20% ]
           │                          │                            │
           └──────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┘
                                      ▼
                        Clay Furnace Crucible @ 1200°C
                                      ▼
                         MOLTEN BOROSILICATE GLASS
                                      ▼
                  [Blowpipe] ➔ Beakers, Retorts, Test Tubes
                  [Grinding] ➔ Convex Eyeglass Lenses (Suika's Glasses)
```

1. **Silica Source ($SiO_2$)**: Crushed quartz rock or white riverbed sand. Melting point of pure quartz is $1,713^\circ\text{C}$ (impossibly hot for a primitive wood fire).
2. **The Flux Catalyst (Soda Ash, $Na_2CO_3$)**: Burning marine kelp or seaweed produces ash extraordinarily rich in sodium carbonate. Mixing 20% soda ash into the quartz sand lowers the melting point dramatically from $1,713^\circ\text{C}$ down to **$1,100\text{--}1,200^\circ\text{C}$**, well within reach of a charcoal furnace boosted by foot bellows.
3. **The Stabilizer (Calcium Carbonate, $CaCO_3$)**: Pure soda-silica glass is water-soluble ("water glass"). Adding 10% crushed, roasted sea shells or limestone introduces calcium oxide ($CaO$), rendering the cooled glass completely insoluble in water and immune to acidic attack.
4. **Shaping & Annealing**:
   - The molten glass gathers on the end of a hollow iron pipe. Rolling on a flat stone (marver) and gently blowing air expands it into flasks, condensers, and retorts.
   - **Annealing**: Hot glass must cool slowly over 24 hours inside warm ash; rapid cooling induces catastrophic internal tensile stress, causing the glass to shatter upon the slightest thermal shock.
   - **Corrective Lenses**: Grinding thick disks of cooled glass against curved stone molds using fine emery/quartz abrasive powder produces precision convex lenses—the exact breakthrough Senku uses to correct Suika's severe myopia ("fuzzy disease") and build astronomical telescopes.

### Section 3: The 25-Step Roadmap to Sulfanilamide (The Sulfa Drug)

The dramatic climax of Senku's initial campaign is curing Ruri's lethal bacterial illness (pneumonia). Because penicillium mold yields in the wild are miniscule and volatile, Senku chooses to synthesize **Sulfanilamide**—the first chemical antibacterial antibiotic discovered in 1935.

```
       THE 25-STEP SULFANILAMIDE ROADMAP (SENKU'S LAB CHART)
       
  [VOLCANO]                 [SEASHORE]               [COAL / WOOD]
  Sulfuric Acid (H2SO4)     Salt Brine (NaCl)        Destructive Distillation
       │                         │                        │
       │                         ▼ (Electrolysis)         ▼
       │                    Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH)   Coal Tar
       │                    + Chlorine Gas (Cl2)          │
       │                         │                        ▼
       │                         ▼                    Benzene (C6H6)
       │                    Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)       │
       │                         │                        ▼
       │                         ▼                    Aniline (C6H5NH2)
       │                    Urea Distillation             │
       │                         │                        ▼
       │                         ▼                    Acetanilide
       │                    Ammonia (NH3)                 │
       │                         │                        │
       └─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┘
                                 ▼
                     Chlorosulfonic Acid Route
                                 ▼
                    p-Acetamidobenzenesulfonyl Chloride
                                 ▼
                           SULFANILAMIDE
                   (Cure for Bacterial Pneumonia)
```

#### Step-by-Step Chemical Transformations:
1. **Sulfuric Acid ($H_2SO_4$) — "The Queen of Chemicals"**:
   - Harvested from natural acidic volcanic hot springs or by roasting iron pyrites ($FeS_2$, "Fool's Gold"):
     $$4FeS_2 + 11O_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2Fe_2O_3 + 8SO_2 \uparrow$$
   - Bubbling sulfur dioxide ($SO_2$) through water with nitrogen oxide catalysts yields dilute sulfuric acid, concentrated by boiling off water until reaching dense, oily $98\%\ H_2SO_4$.
2. **Salt Brine Electrolysis ($NaCl + H_2O$)**:
   - Running direct electrical current from a copper-magneto generator through salt water with carbon electrodes yields three vital industrial precursors simultaneously:
     $$2NaCl + 2H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{Electricity}} 2NaOH + Cl_2 \uparrow + H_2 \uparrow$$
   - Combining hydrogen ($H_2$) and chlorine ($Cl_2$) gases and dissolving in water yields **Hydrochloric Acid ($HCl$)**.
3. **Ammonia ($NH_3$) Synthesis**:
   - Synthesized by collecting animal/human urine, boiling off stale urea with slaked lime ($Ca(OH)_2$):
     $$(NH_2)_2CO + Ca(OH)_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} CaCO_3 + 2NH_3 \uparrow$$
4. **Coal Tar Distillation to Aniline ($C_6H_5NH_2$)**:
   - Heating coal or bituminous wood in an airtight clay retort (destructive distillation) yields black, viscous coal tar.
   - Fractional distillation of coal tar yields **Benzene ($C_6H_6$)**.
   - Nitrating benzene with mixed nitric/sulfuric acid yields **Nitrobenzene ($C_6H_5NO_2$)**.
   - Reducing nitrobenzene with scrap iron and hydrochloric acid yields **Aniline ($C_6H_5NH_2$)**.
5. **Acetanilide & Chlorosulfonation**:
   - Reacting aniline with acetic acid (concentrated vinegar) yields white crystalline **Acetanilide**, protecting the delicate amino group.
   - Treating acetanilide with chlorosulfonic acid produces *p-acetamidobenzenesulfonyl chloride*.
6. **Final Amination & Hydrolysis to Sulfanilamide**:
   - Treating the chloride intermediate with concentrated ammonia ($NH_3$) attaches the sulfonamide group.
   - Boiling in dilute hydrochloric acid removes the protective acetyl group, yielding pure white crystals of **Sulfanilamide ($C_6H_8N_2O_2S$)**.
   - **Biological Mechanism**: Sulfanilamide is a structural analog to para-aminobenzoic acid (PABA). Pathogenic bacteria absorb the false drug, irreversibly blocking their enzymatic synthesis of folic acid, halting bacterial cell division and eradicating pneumonia.

### Section 4: The Electric Age — Copper Dynamos & Edison Light Bulbs

To illuminate darkness and power industrial chemical electrolysis, Senku transitions the Kingdom of Science from thermal muscle power to the electromagnetic spectrum.

```
       FARADAY'S LAW MANUAL GENERATOR (THE WATER-WHEEL DYNAMO)
       
              Stream Current
                    │
                    ▼
          [Overhead Water Wheel]
                    │ (Wooden Spur Gears 1:10 Ratio)
                    ▼
    Rotating Shaft with Magnetite Lodestones
                    │
                    ▼ (Continuous Magnetic Flux Cut, dΦ/dt)
         [Coiled Copper Wire Core] ───────► Direct Current (DC) Electricity
```

#### 1. Copper Wire Drawing & Insulation
- **Smelting Copper**: Smelt green malachite ore ($Cu_2CO_3(OH)_2$) in a charcoal furnace. Cast raw copper into thin cylindrical rods.
- **Wire Drawing**: Forge a thick iron plate drilled with a sequence of progressively smaller tapered holes. Coat copper rods in animal fat and forcibly draw them through the die using a heavy wooden lever winch. Stretches thick copper ingots into hundreds of meters of uniform wire.
- **Electrical Insulation**: Raw copper wires short-circuit upon touching. Coat the wire in the sap of the Japanese lacquer tree (*Toxicodendron vernicifluum*, **urushi**) or molten beeswax. Urushi polymerizes into an indestructible, heat-resistant, non-conductive dielectric enamel.

#### 2. The Magneto Generator
- According to **Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction**:
  $$\mathcal{E} = -N \frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$$
- Winding insulated copper wire hundreds of times around two iron cores creates stator coils.
- Mounting natural permanent lodestones (magnetite, $Fe_3O_4$) onto a rotating wooden disk spun by hand or water wheel rapidly changes magnetic flux through the coils, generating steady **12 to 24 Volt Direct Current (DC)** power.

#### 3. The Bamboo Filament Incandescent Bulb
- To generate electric light, take a curved glass tube.
- Carve a paper-thin hair filament from high-silica mountain bamboo.
- Carbonize the bamboo strip by baking it inside an airtight clay crucible filled with charcoal powder (turning organic cellulose into pure crystalline carbon graphite).
- Mount the carbon filament inside the glass bulb. Evacuate all atmospheric oxygen by igniting a speck of red phosphorus inside or pumping with a mercury column.
- Pass electric current: resistance heats the carbon filament to **$2,000^\circ\text{C}$**, glowing with brilliant incandescent white light without burning, because zero oxygen remains inside the evacuated glass envelope.

### Section 5: The Wireless Transceiver (Cell Phone Blueprint)

The pinnacle of Senku's early engineering is breaking the barrier of physical distance: transmitting the human voice across miles of wilderness using wireless high-frequency electromagnetic radiation.

```
       PRIMITIVE VACUUM TUBE AM TRANSMITTER SCHEMATIC
       
    Microphone (Carbon Granules)
           │
           ▼ (Audio Voltage Modulator)
     [Control Grid]
           │
  Cathode ───► Heated Filament Emits Electrons ───► Anode Plate (+100V)
           │
           ▼ (High-Frequency Carrier from Quartz Crystal Oscillator)
     [Tank Circuit: Copper Coil + Foil Capacitor]
           │
           ▼
    Long-Wire Aerial Antenna ══════════► 1000 kHz Radio Wave Emission
```

1. **The Vacuum Tube Triode**:
   - The heart of telecommunications is the **triode amplifier**.
   - Inside an evacuated glass bulb, a heated filament (cathode) boils off a cloud of free electrons (Edison-Richardson effect).
   - Electrons are attracted across the vacuum gap to a positively charged metal plate (anode).
   - Inserting a fine wire mesh (**the control grid**) between cathode and plate allows tiny, weak input voltages (from a carbon microphone) to throttle the massive flow of plate current, amplifying weak signals by orders of magnitude.
2. **The Quartz Crystal Oscillator**:
   - Slicing and polishing a natural quartz crystal to exact physical dimensions exploits the **piezoelectric effect**: applying voltage causes the crystal to vibrate at a precise resonant frequency (e.g., $1.5\text{ MHz}$).
   - This resonant oscillation feeds the vacuum tube circuit, generating an unyielding high-frequency radio carrier wave.
3. **The Carbon Granule Microphone**:
   - Roasting coal or bread crumbs into fine conductive carbon grains packed inside an animal bladder or thin diaphragm.
   - When speech sound waves strike the diaphragm, they compress the carbon grains, varying electrical resistance in direct proportion to human voice frequencies, modulating the radio carrier wave for crystal radio receivers miles away.
